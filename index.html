Why data and AI, and why now

I didn't choose this because it's popular.

I got here because I wanted to build things that solve real problems — not just things that can be built. Data analysis, machine learning and embedded systems are tools I use to understand problems and develop practical solutions.

My long-term interest is healthcare technology. I've always cared about medicine, but my strengths sit in mathematics, engineering and problem-solving — so I'm approaching healthcare through technology instead of around it.
