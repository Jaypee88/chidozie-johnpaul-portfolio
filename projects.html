Flood ESC — Flood Prediction Model

The ML component of a wider flood prediction & emergency-response system

Built as part of a multidisciplinary team for the IEEE Request & Response Challenge. I owned the machine learning side: sourcing and preparing dynamic and static environmental data — including elevation and satellite data through Google Earth Engine — then training and evaluating the model in Google Colab on GPU. Once it was ready, I handed it off to the software engineering team, who integrated it into the full application. The project cleared the first round of the challenge.
